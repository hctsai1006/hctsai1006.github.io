User Guide
  1. Drag the mouse to rotate the view.
  2. Use the mouse wheel to zoom; double-click a target to move the camera to it.
  3. Press R to return to the origin (Earth).
  4. Adjust the "G mag maximum" slider on the top-right to filter stars by brightness.
  5. Hover a star to show the tooltip; click to open detailed info.
  6. Use "Create My Planet" (bottom-left) to create a new planet; prediction results will appear next.
  7. Press Esc, click the backdrop, or use the top-right close button to exit.
